Use case: game texture. Generate one square seamless texture of lightly weathered medium warm gray cast-concrete with fine natural aggregate, subtle pale mineral flecks and restrained irregular tonal variations. This is the shared stone/concrete material for realistic high-resolution isometric road tunnel portals in a city-building game, to complement finely textured neutral-gray asphalt and OpenGFX2-quality painted transport graphics. View directly overhead, no perspective. Uniform ambient lighting, no gradient, spotlight, dark edges, vignette, shadows, bevels or direction of light. No road, tunnel, arch, building, bricks, mortar lines, borders, text or objects: only continuous concrete texture across the full image. Fine texture at the scale of a 128-pixel-wide sprite, not big stones or large cracks. Rich but subtle game-art surface, not cartoon, not plastic, not rough gravel. Opposite edges should tile seamlessly. Base color approximately RGB 148,145,136. No transparency.
